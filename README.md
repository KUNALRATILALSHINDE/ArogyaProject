# 🌿 Arogya Health – Your Personalized Wellness Companion

Welcome to **Arogya Health**, an interactive wellness platform designed to guide users toward a healthier lifestyle using the ancient wisdom of Yoga and Ayurveda, blended with the power of modern technology. 🧘‍♂️💡

Whether you're a curious beginner, a mental fitness enthusiast, or someone looking for holistic recommendations — **Arogya Health has something for you**!

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 🧘 Yoga Explorer  
Dive into the world of Yoga! Discover various poses, their benefits, and how they positively impact your body and mind.

- Detailed yoga postures with visuals & descriptions
- Filter by difficulty or body focus (e.g., core, flexibility)
- Learn how each pose benefits physical and mental health

### 🏥 Prakriti Tests (Ayurveda Body Type Quiz)  
Take an Ayurvedic-inspired test to determine your unique body type (Vata, Pitta, or Kapha) and receive lifestyle suggestions tailored to your constitution.

- Simple, guided quiz interface
- Personalized result with diet, exercise, and daily habits suggestions
- ML-backed recommendation engine

### 🎮 Brain Games  
Engage your brain and boost cognitive fitness with fun, interactive games.

- Puzzle-based games to test memory, logic, and focus
- Progress tracker to monitor improvement over time
- Lightweight and fun UI

### 🤖 Smart Health Chatbot  
Got questions? Our friendly chatbot is trained on health and wellness data to offer instant responses and smart suggestions.

- Real-time Q&A
- Personalized responses based on user data
- Integrated with ML models for recommendations
